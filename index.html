The 5 Pillars of Mastery

Every single discipline of modern video creation engineered with mathematical precision, artistic taste, and developer automation.

01

Video Editing & Audience Retention Psychology

Video editing is the engineering of human attention. Every cut, transition, and micro-zoom is calibrated against viewer drop-off analytics and pattern interrupt theory.

  • The 3-Second Hook Architecture: Visual, sonic, and curiosity-driven hooks designed to stop the scroll on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  • James Jani-Grade Documentary Pacing: Multi-act narrative structuring for 15–60 minute deep dives, chapter transitions, retention resets, and tension curves.
  • Cinema-Grade Color Grading: DaVinci Resolve color pipelines with Kodak 2383 film stock emulation, balanced skin tones, and rich contrast control.
02

Motion Graphics & Kinetic Typography

Graphics that drive comprehension. From high-contrast editorial bold titles to seamless 2.5D camera maps, every pixel moves with organic intention.

  • 2.5D Photo Parallax: Depth-mapping archival photos with 3D camera fly-throughs, smoke, and particulate embers.
  • Cubic Bezier Easing: Custom graph editor curves with heavy ease-out cushions. Zero linear keyframes.
  • Rotoscoped Stacking: Layering kinetic text behind speakers for cinematic 3D depth.
03

3D CGI & Coded Motion

Where technical precision meets artistic polish. Building 3D terrain cross-sections in Blender and programmatic, pixel-perfect financial data charts using Remotion (React / TypeScript).

  • 3D GIS Elevation: Realistic USGS bathymetric models and canyon terrain simulations in Blender Cycles.
  • Remotion Programmatic Animation: Rendering deterministic data charts, yield curves, and interactive exhibits via code.
  • Apple & Anthropic Grade Polish: Calm luxury motion, generous whitespace, and restrained kinetic typography.
04

AI Video Creations & Generative Workflows

Superpowering production through artificial intelligence without sacrificing human taste. Studio-lit avatars, generative b-roll, and natural speech synthesis.

  • Photorealistic AI Avatars: Studio-lit HeyGen & Seedance presenter integration with flawless lip-sync and micro-gestures.
  • Generative Cinematic B-Roll: Custom-prompted scenes in Runway Gen-3 and Midjourney, graded to match live documentary stock.
  • The Anti-AI Protocol: 35mm grain matching, chromatic aberration, and authentic foley audio so viewers never spot the AI.
05

Custom Pipeline Automation

The developer-editor advantage. Using Python and pycapcut, I auto-assemble timelines from scripts and Whisper SRTs, eliminating 80% of repetitive assembly work.

  • Deterministic CapCut Assembly: Auto-segments voiceover audio and generates ready-to-polish NLE project files automatically.
  • Whisper SRT Word Alignment: Millisecond-accurate word timing that syncs subtitles and B-roll to exact vocal pauses.
  • Batch Hardware NVENC Rendering: GPU-accelerated rendering pipelines with deterministic color space tags.
