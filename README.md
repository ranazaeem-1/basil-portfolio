## 🛠️ The 5 Pillars of Expertise

- **Video Editing & Retention Psychology**: 3-second hook architecture, pattern interrupts every 2.5–3.5s, multi-act long-form documentary pacing, DaVinci Resolve color grading.
- **Motion Graphics & Kinetic Typography**: Archival photo depth mapping, fluid cubic-bezier curves, glassmorphic lower thirds, and tactical HUD telemetry.
- **3D CGI & Coded Motion**: Blender 3D bathymetric terrain cross-sections (The Hydraulic Record) and programmatic data visualization with Remotion (React / TypeScript).
- **AI Video Generation & Avatars**: Studio-lit AI presenter integration (HeyGen, Seedance), generative B-roll (Runway Gen-3, Midjourney, Luma), ElevenLabs vocal mastering, and OpenAI Whisper SRT alignment.
- **Developer-Grade Pipeline Automation**: Proprietary Python automation (`pycapcut`, `build_draft.py`) that auto-assembles full NLE timelines from voiceovers and placement specs.
